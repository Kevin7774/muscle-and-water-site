Muscle & Water

支持

Muscle & Water 没有账号，也没有服务器。大部分问题在这里能找到答案。

记录存在哪里？

只存在你的手机上。记录会跟着手机本身的备份（iCloud 或电脑）走；照片记录不进备份。

换手机怎么办？

用 iCloud 或电脑把旧手机备份、恢复到新手机，记录会一起过去。照片记录不进备份，换手机前在照片记录里把照片一张张导出到系统相册。

重装或换手机后，Plus 不见了？

点右上角「···」→「会员 Plus」→「恢复购买」。要登录购买时用的那个 Apple 账号。

怎么取消订阅？

打开 iPhone 的「设置」，点最上面你的名字，再点「订阅」，选 Muscle & Water。在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，下一期就不会扣款。

照镜子一直不开始？

五项检查都亮了才开始数三秒：肩膀到腰都在画面里、站着、手离开腰、两肩放平、光线够。把手机放稳，往后退一点，面向光。也可以直接选一张半身或全身照。

读不到睡眠？

先在 App 的「设置」里打开「从健康 App 读睡眠」。以前拒绝过的话，到 iPhone 的「设置」→「健康」→「数据访问与设备」→ Muscle & Water，允许读取睡眠。手表或手机要记过昨晚的睡眠才读得到。

怎么删除所有数据？

点右上角「···」→「设置」→「删除全部数据」。记录和照片都会删掉，不能恢复。删掉 App 也会一起删掉。

Support

Muscle & Water has no account and no server. Most answers are here.

Where are my logs stored?

Only on your phone. Logs are included in your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer). The photo record is not backed up.

I'm switching to a new phone.

Back up the old phone to iCloud or a computer and restore it to the new one. Your logs come along. The photo record isn't in the backup, so before you switch, export each photo from the photo record to your photo library.

Plus is gone after reinstalling or switching phones.

Tap ··· at the top right, then Plus membership, then Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and choose Muscle & Water. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends and the next one won't be charged.

The mirror check won't start.

The three-second count starts only when all five checks light up: shoulders to waist in frame, standing, hands off your waist, shoulders level, enough light. Rest the phone somewhere steady, step back a little and face the light. You can also pick a half-body or full-body photo instead.

Sleep isn't showing up.

First turn on Read sleep from Health in the app's Settings. If you said no before, go to Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Muscle & Water on your iPhone and allow sleep. Your watch or phone also needs to have recorded last night's sleep.

How do I delete all my data?

Tap ··· at the top right, then Settings, then Delete all data. Logs and photos are deleted and can't be recovered. Deleting the app deletes them too.
